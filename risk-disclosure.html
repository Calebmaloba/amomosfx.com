Risk disclosure

Trading is not suitable for everyone. Understand the risks before opening an account or placing a trade.

!

You can lose money.

Trading leveraged products and complex derivatives can lead to rapid losses. You may lose some or all of the money you commit, depending on the product and its terms.

General financial risk

Prices can move quickly and unpredictably due to economic news, market sentiment, liquidity and other events. Past performance, historical patterns, simulations and demo results do not guarantee future outcomes.

Leverage and complex products

Leverage can magnify both gains and losses. Some products have complex pricing or settlement rules. Before trading, read the provider's product disclosure, fees, margin requirements and terms, and ensure you understand how losses may arise.

No financial advice or profit guarantee

AmomosFX provides general educational information only. Content on this site is not personalised financial, investment, tax or legal advice and is not a recommendation to buy, sell or hold any asset. We do not guarantee returns, signals, accuracy of outcomes or profitability.

Trade responsibly

  • Never trade money needed for rent, food, bills or other essential expenses.
  • Do not borrow money to trade.
  • Understand the product and the possibility of losing your full amount at risk.
  • Consider independent professional advice if you are unsure.
  • Check that the provider is authorised to offer the relevant service in your jurisdiction.

Provider-specific warnings

If you follow a link to Deriv or another provider, review the current risk warning and product terms on that provider's own website before proceeding. A broker referral does not mean that AmomosFX guarantees, endorses or controls that provider's trading outcomes.
