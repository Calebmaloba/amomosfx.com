MARKET INSIGHTS

Think clearly about the markets.

Use these educational notes to understand what traders watch. They are not live prices, forecasts or trade signals.

Important: This starter site does not connect to live market data. The information below is general education, not current market analysis or a recommendation to trade.
◎MACROECONOMICS

Why interest rates matter

Central bank decisions can influence borrowing costs, currencies, bond yields and investor expectations. Markets may react to the difference between an announcement and what was expected.

Study question: Where can you find official central bank announcements?

⌁TECHNICAL ANALYSIS

Support and resistance

Some traders watch price areas where buying or selling previously increased. These areas are subjective and can fail; they should never be treated as guaranteed turning points.

Study question: How might you test whether a chart observation is repeatable?

◇RISK AWARENESS

Volatility and position size

Volatility describes how much prices fluctuate. A volatile market can move quickly in either direction. Position sizing and stop orders have limitations and cannot guarantee a particular exit price in all conditions.
