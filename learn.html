LEARNING PATH

A sensible place to start

These articles are educational summaries, not a recommendation to buy or sell any financial product.

01

BEGINNER · 5 MIN

What is trading?

Trading involves buying and selling financial instruments in an attempt to benefit from price changes. Prices can move against you, and a trade can lose money. Learn how different markets work before participating.

02

BEGINNER · 6 MIN

Understanding charts

A price chart shows how a market has moved over a selected period. Candlestick charts commonly show an opening price, closing price, high and low for each period. Past chart patterns do not reliably predict future results.

03

BEGINNER · 7 MIN

What are leverage and margin?

Leverage lets a trader control a larger exposure than the amount initially committed. It can magnify gains and losses. Margin is collateral required to maintain certain positions. Make sure you understand the product rules and possible liquidation before trading.

04

PRACTICAL · 5 MIN

Build a risk plan

Consider your goals, time horizon, maximum acceptable loss and the risks of each product. Avoid borrowing to trade, do not risk essential living expenses and take breaks when emotions affect decisions. No risk plan can eliminate loss.

05

PRACTICAL · 4 MIN

Practise with a demo account

A demo account can help you learn an interface using simulated funds. Demo performance may differ from real trading due to execution, emotions and market conditions. Do not assume success in a demo guarantees real-money success.
