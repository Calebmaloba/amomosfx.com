ABOUT US

Education before action.

AmomosFX is being built as an accessible place for people to learn about financial markets and trading risks.

Our purpose

Our goal is to present basic trading concepts in clear language, encourage careful research and make risk awareness a central part of learning.

What we provide

  • General educational articles and learning resources.
  • Explanations of market concepts and risk management.
  • Links to external resources that visitors can research independently.

What we are not

AmomosFX is not a bank, broker, investment adviser or guarantor of trading results. We do not accept deposits or hold client trading funds through this website. We do not provide personalised investment advice.
